Sudoku

Fill the grid so that every row, every column and every 3×3 box holds the numbers 1 to 9 exactly once. The dark printed numbers are clues and can't be changed.

Every puzzle here has exactly one solution and can be cracked by logic alone, so you never need to guess. Your grid is checked against the rules, not against an answer key: the moment every row, column and box is complete and clash-free, it's solved.

Playing

  • Tap a square, then tap a number. Numbers that clash with another in the same row, column or box turn red.
  • Notes switches the number pad to pencil marks, for keeping track of what a square could still be. Fill notes pencils in every possibility at once.
  • Erase clears a square, Undo steps back one move (as many as you like).
  • Tap the clock to pause. It also stops whenever you leave the page, and your puzzle is saved as you go, so you can close the tab and pick up later.

Hints

A hint finds the next move a person would make and explains it instead of just giving it away. First it tells you where to look. Tap Show me to see the reasoning laid out on the board: green numbers are the possibilities that matter, red crossed-out ones are being ruled out. Fill it in places the number for you. If you've put down a number that can't be part of any finished grid, the hint points that out first.

Hints are unlimited. Your best time only counts puzzles solved without one. Hints name squares by row and column: R3C5 is row 3 from the top, column 5 from the left.

Difficulty

Levels are graded by the techniques a puzzle actually needs, not just by how many clues it has.

  • Easy: every number can be found by scanning for the one spot it fits.
  • Medium: fewer clues, and some squares only give way once you count what they can still hold.
  • Hard: you'll need notes. Pointing pairs, box-line reductions, naked and hidden pairs and triples.
  • Expert: X-Wings, Swordfish and XY-Wings, on top of everything above.

Keyboard

1-9 fill in, Shift+number pencils a note, arrows move, Backspace erases, N notes, H hint, U or Ctrl+Z undo, P pause.
